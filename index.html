How it works

  1. Choose niche, goal, platform and posting speed, then make the plan.
  2. Every post day has a format (Reel, Carousel, Story, Post, Live), an idea, a hook, a caption draft and hashtags.
  3. Tap Swap idea if a day does not fit. Edit the caption. Tick Done after posting.
  4. Export to Excel to share with your team or print it.

Honest note: this is a template-based planner. It does not know live trends or your analytics. Best-time hints are starting points only, so check your own Insights. Use only real photos, real numbers and real customer feedback with permission.
